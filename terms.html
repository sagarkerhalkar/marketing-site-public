2. The service

Sagar RMM lets your organization monitor, maintain, update, back up and support its computers from one console. It includes the server software, the agent installed on each PC, and our support. It runs either on your own server, or on a server we host for you. The features in each plan are listed on our pricing.

3. Free pilot

  • The pilot is free for 30 days on up to 25 PCs, with the features of the Pro plan, and you have no obligation to buy.
  • At the end we give you a report of what the system found and fixed.
  • If you do not continue, uninstall the agent from your PCs (we will help). Data on a server we host is deleted within 30 days.
  • We may end a pilot early if it is misused.

4. Plans and payment

  • Prices are per PC per month, as shown on our pricing or in your quotation, plus GST at the applicable rate.
  • We bill monthly in advance for the number of PCs with the agent installed on the billing date. PCs added during a month are billed from the next month.
  • Invoices are payable within 15 days by UPI or bank transfer. If you pay for a year in advance, you get two months free.
  • We tell you at least 30 days before a price change, and it applies from your next billing period.
  • Fees already paid are not refunded, except where we fail to provide the service (see section 13), or where the law requires a refund.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Install the agent only on computers your organization owns or is legally allowed to manage.
  • Tell the people who use those computers, in advance and in plain language, what Sagar RMM collects and can do. This includes monitoring of websites and apps, remote access and backups. Rely on a valid legal ground under the DPDP Act, such as consent or a legitimate use it allows. The Privacy policy explains these features.
  • Choose the remote-access setting that suits your organization. By default remote control connects without asking the user. If staff must approve first, choose an "ask" setting.
  • Where children use your computers (for example in a school), follow the DPDP Act's rules for children's data, and switch off or limit features as needed.
  • Give access to the console only to people who need it, with their own accounts and two-step sign-in, and keep passwords and the backup password safe.
  • Do not use Sagar RMM to break the law, to spy on anyone without a lawful basis, to attack other systems, or to copy, resell or reverse-engineer the software (except through our Partner plan, or as the law allows).
  • If you run Sagar RMM on your own server, keep that server, its operating system and its network secure, powered and connected to the internet so it can update.

7. Your data

You own all data that your computers and people put into Sagar RMM. You give us permission to process it only as needed to run the service for you. We may use counts and measurements that cannot identify you or any person (for example, how many updates fail across all customers) to improve the product. You can export your data at any time while you use the service, and for 30 days after it ends.

8. Backups

Backups are encrypted on each PC and stored in cloud storage that you choose and pay for. You are responsible for that storage account, its limits and its cost, and for keeping the backup password safe. Without the password, nobody, including us, can read the backups. Backups can fail, for example when a PC is off, its disk is failing or the storage is full. The console shows each PC's backup state, so check it and test a restore from time to time.

9. Updates

We improve Sagar RMM regularly. Servers check for updates and install them automatically, and the agent on each PC updates itself. We try to avoid interrupting work, and an update waits while a remote session is open. We may change or remove features. If we remove a major feature that you are paying for, we tell you in advance, and you may end the service without further charge.
