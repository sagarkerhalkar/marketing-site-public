What owners and CTOs ask us.

Does our data leave our office?

Not if you choose your own server. PC data stays on your server and backups go to cloud storage in your own account. A hosted option is there for those who prefer it.

How do we know it is worth the money?

Run the free 30-day pilot on 25 PCs. The 30-day report shows every problem, its cause and what was repaired, so you decide on your own results.

Can staff misuse remote access?

Each person gets only the rights you give them. Remote control can ask the user first, needs two-step sign-in, and every session is recorded with the technician's name.

We already have an IT team. Why this?

So they spend the day on your business instead of fire-fighting. Repairs, updates and backups run at night, and the team starts the morning with a short list.

How long does rollout take?

One small installer per PC, or pushed to the whole network. Each PC appears within five minutes. Most offices are running in an afternoon.

Will it slow our computers down?

No. The agent uses a small share of the processor, and repairs and updates wait until after working hours.

Can we sell it under our own name?

Yes, with the Partner plan: your name and logo, all your customers in one console, and your own price.
