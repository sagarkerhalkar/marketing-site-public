Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short

  • On this website we only collect what you type into the demo form, to call you back.
  • Inside Sagar RMM, your organization decides what is collected about its computers and staff. We process it only to run the service for you.
  • Backups are encrypted on the PC before they leave it and go to storage your organization owns.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Monitoring features: what organizations must do

Some features can show an organization how its computers are used. They are switched on or off by the organization, not by us.

  • Websites visited and apps used are recorded by default and can be switched off in the console. Switching "apps used" off deletes what it collected. Switching "websites visited" off stops new records, and older ones are deleted after 180 days. The staff portal on each PC shows that web activity is monitored by IT.
  • Remote access can be set for each PC or department: switched off, ask the user before viewing, ask the user before taking control, or connect without asking. Out of the box it connects without asking. Organizations that want staff to approve first must choose an "ask" setting. Every session is recorded in the audit log.

Under the DPDP Act the organization, as data fiduciary, must tell its staff (and students, where relevant) what is collected and why, and rely on a valid legal ground such as consent or a legitimate use the Act allows. We give organizations the settings and notices to do this, but the decision and the duty are theirs.

6. Where the data is kept

  • On the organization's own server: most organizations run Sagar RMM on their own server. The data then stays on that server, and we see it only when the organization asks us for support.
  • Hosted by us: if we run the server for an organization, we tell them where it is located before they sign up, and we keep their data separate from every other organization's.
  • Backups are encrypted on the PC before they leave it, with AES-256-GCM. They go to cloud storage that the organization owns and chooses (Google Drive, OneDrive or an S3-compatible service such as Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2 or Wasabi). The backup password is held on the organization's Sagar RMM server, and the organization can view it to keep a copy.
  • The "Ask" assistant answers from the organization's own data. If AI answers are switched on, the AI model runs on the organization's own network, and the app refuses to send this data to any outside AI service.

8. How long data is kept

  • PC history (health readings, event logs, alerts): 180 days by default. Each organization can choose from 1 day to 360 days in its settings.
  • Websites visited and apps used: 180 days. Switching "apps used" off deletes its records at once.
  • Backups: as many copies as the organization chooses (for example 7 daily, 4 weekly and 6 monthly), in its own storage.
  • When an organization stops using Sagar RMM: on a server we host, we delete its data within 30 days of the end of the service, after giving it the chance to export, unless the law requires us to keep something longer. On the organization's own server, the organization deletes it.
  • Demo requests sent through this website: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless you become a customer or ask us to delete them sooner.

Where Indian law requires certain records or logs to be kept for a minimum period, we keep them for that period.

9. Security

  • The console is reached over HTTPS, and each person signs in with their own account. Two-step sign-in is available.
  • Roles limit what each administrator can see and do, down to particular departments or PCs.
  • Every remote session and change made in the console is recorded with the person's name.
  • Stored secrets, such as storage keys, are encrypted on the server. Backups are encrypted before they leave the PC.
  • Organizations running their own server are responsible for securing that server and its network.

10. If something goes wrong

If we learn of a breach affecting personal data we hold or process, we tell the affected organization without delay. We give it what we know about what happened, the data involved, and what we are doing about it, so it can meet its own duties to inform the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected. Where we are the data fiduciary (for example for demo requests), we inform the Board and the people affected ourselves, as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask for a summary of the personal data held about you and how it is used, ask for it to be corrected, completed or erased, withdraw consent you gave, name a nominee, and complain about how it is handled.

  • About data inside Sagar RMM: contact the organization that manages your computer. If you contact us instead, we pass your request to that organization and help it answer.
  • About anything you sent us through this website: contact us directly (section 14).

We acknowledge requests within 2 working days and answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Children

Sagar RMM is sold to organizations, not to individuals, and this website is not meant for children. Some customers, such as schools, use it on computers that children use. Such organizations must make sure that their use of monitoring features follows the DPDP Act's rules for children's personal data. We can help them switch off or limit those features.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how customer data is handled, we tell customers by email or WhatsApp before it takes effect.
